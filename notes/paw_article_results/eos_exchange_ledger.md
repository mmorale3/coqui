# Si PAW EOS: term-by-term cross-code energy ledger

Started 2026-07-29, after [[project_paw_rpa_instability_is_coqui_side]] fixed the
RPA correlation (`V_LL` transpose, commit 86ace47) and exposed a *second*,
previously masked defect: with `E_c` now matching ABINIT, the Si EXX+RPA total
energy has **no minimum** anywhere in `a = 10.05..10.55` Bohr.

Goal: debug the one-body, Hartree and exchange energies against an instrumented
ABINIT as a function of lattice constant, and fix the EOS.

---

## §1 Phase 0 — the baseline, and why the pipeline is not the problem

The decisive control was already on disk: `~/ceph/CoQui/abinit/eos_nc500_coqui`
is a **norm-conserving** (`Si_GGA_noNLCC.psp8`) 6-volume series through the
*identical* pipeline (same ABINIT → `abinit2coqui` → CoQui THC-RPA path, same
`div_treatment = gygi`, same fitter).

| series | a0 (Bohr) | B0 (GPa) | B' | BM resid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NC (eos_nc500_coqui)** | **10.2259** | **101.1** | 4.08 | 0.008 mHa |
| PAW jth_with_d (eos_exxrpa) | *no minimum in range* | — | — | — |
| VASP/PAW RPA@PBE (Harl 2010) | 10.244 | 98 | — | — |

The NC series reproduces the literature reference. **The pipeline, the Ewald
convention, the fitter and the RPA correlation are all sound; the defect is
specific to the PAW path.**

### Where the PAW defect sits

Term slopes at `a = 10.25` (Ha/Bohr, central difference):

| term | NC | PAW | PAW − NC |
|---|---|---|---|
| E_1e | −1.10886 | −1.14382 | **−0.03496** |
| E_H | +0.12511 | +0.12105 | −0.00406 |
| E_x | +0.15613 | +0.15118 | −0.00495 |
| E_c | +0.00899 | +0.00815 | −0.00084 |
| Ewald | +0.82044 | +0.82044 | 0 |
| **TOTAL** | **+0.00180** | **−0.04300** | **−0.04480** |

The PAW−NC *total* drifts −22.3 mHa across `a = 10.05..10.55`, and **78 % of
that drift is in E_1e** (−17.5 mHa), the rest split between E_x (−2.4 mHa) and
E_H (−2.0 mHa). Two different pseudopotentials are allowed to differ in each
term's absolute value, but for two datasets describing the same valence physics
their *difference* must be flat in volume; a −0.035 Ha/Bohr slope difference in
E_1e is ~35× what a good-pseudo pair should show.

Note the curvature is already right: PAW `d²E/da² = +0.0827` at `a = 10.25`
against `+0.0768` expected for B0 = 98 GPa. **Only the slope is wrong**, by
+0.043 Ha/Bohr, i.e. the defect is nearly linear in `a` over this range.

### Two hypotheses killed before any new run

1. **Missing exchange divergence correction.** The needed slope repair
   (+0.0430 Ha/Bohr at `a = 10.25`) numerically equals `d/da` of a `−C/a` term
   with `C` = the measured 0.4495 Ha Gygi correction (+0.0441). But the runs
   *do* request `div_treatment = gygi` / `hf_div_treatment = gygi`, and the
   stored Madelung constant scales exactly as 1/a: `madelung × a = 1.14622` at
   **all six** volumes. The correction is present and volume-correct.
   Its analytic value, `−madelung·N_el/2 = −0.4473 Ha` at `a = 10.25`, was
   verified against the code path (`HF_K_correction = −madelung·S·Dm·S`,
   contracted with an S-idempotent γ).
2. **Wrongly-scaling α_Z / G=0 local-potential term.** `pp_local(G=0)·Ω =
   50.264012` is *bit-constant* across all six volumes, so the 1/Ω scaling of
   that term is right too. (Whether the *constant* is right is a separate
   question — see §3.)

Fitting the drift to `C/Ω`, `C/a`, `C·Ω`, `C/Ω²` all give ~100 µHa residuals:
over a 5 % range in `a` every form is linear, so **the functional form cannot
localize this defect. Only a term-by-term external reference can.**

## §2 The protocol

One-shot EXX on PBE orbitals: with identical orbitals and identical operators
the two codes must agree term by term, so any term whose *volume derivative*
disagrees is the defect.

### ABINIT side — two datasets per volume, same orbitals

Inputs generated by `gen_eos_ledger.py` into
`~/ceph/CoQui/abinit/eos_ledger_{jthd,nc}/a*`.

- **DS1** `ixc 11` (PBE), converged SCF, `tolvrs 1e-10`. Source of **E_1e and
  E_H**. A normal variational SCF, so its decomposition is trustworthy.
- **DS2** `ixc 40` (pure Fock, α = 1), `nstep 1`, `getwfk`/`getden` from DS1.
  Source of **E_x**.

**Do NOT read E_x from DS2's printed total.** The DC total goes through
`e_eigenvalues`, which comes from one partially converged diagonalization; the
pre-existing `eos_exx500` series shows it jumping non-monotonically by ~100 mHa
across volumes (etotal2 = −9.3556, −9.3332, −9.2244, −9.3353, −9.2757 for
a = 10.15..10.55). The direct (non-DC) decomposition is worse still — one of
those runs printed `kinetic = 419.95 Ha`. Read instead:

- `e_fock0` — the smooth plane-wave Fock energy of the orbitals the Fock
  operator was *built from*, i.e. the PBE orbitals, by construction at step 1.
- `efock` from `pawdenpot` — the one-centre Fock (vv + cv) from the input
  `pawrhoij`. Verified pre-update: DS2's `e1t10` equals DS1's final `e1t10` to
  1.1 µHa, so DS2's single `pawdenpot` call ran on the PBE occupancies.

Measured contamination if one ignores this: DS2's `kinetic` is 3.3883 against
DS1's 3.0458 — the orbitals move by 0.34 Ha worth of kinetic energy in that one
step. `e_fock` (−2.05140) vs `e_fock0` (−2.03975) differ by 11.7 mHa for the
same reason.

### ABINIT instrumentation

`abinit_ene_instr.py` — two env-gated `write` blocks, no computed value touched;
`--revert` restores. Rebuilt to `~/ceph/CoQui/abinit/bin/abinit_ene`.

- `ABI_DUMP_ENE` in `67_common/m_common.F90::prtene` — every field of the
  `energies` container at es24.16, plus both assembled totals. Needed because
  ABINIT prints nothing for `e_fock`/`e_fock0`.
- `ABI_DUMP_PAWENE` in `65_paw/m_paw_denpot.F90::pawdenpot` — the individual
  on-site pieces that get lumped into the single printed `PAW spherical_terms`:
  `e1t10`, `eh2`, `e1xc`, `etild1xc`, `exccore`, `efock`, `efockdc`, `ehnzc`,
  `ekincore`, `eh2dc`. Summed over `my_comm_atom` when atom parallelism is on.

Validation: the fresh DS1 at `a = 10.25` reproduces the EOS campaign's own
`eos_jthd` DS1 to ~2 µHa on every term (kinetic 3.04580891 vs 3.04580903,
corepsp bit-identical, etotal −7.96716509 vs −7.96716281), confirming the
ledger runs share the mf.h5's provenance.

### CoQui side

New `print_e1_decomposition` (`src/methods/SCF/energy_decomposition.hpp`),
called from the rpa/hf driver, splits `e_1e = Tr[Dm·H0]` by feeding
single-term H matrices through the *same* `eval_hf_energy` contraction (so no
prefactor is re-derived — cf. the `rad_fac` and `−1/(4N_k)` history):

- `hamilt::set_kinetic` → `Tr[γT]` (pre-existing `skip_pp` path)
- `hamilt::set_vnl_only` (new) → `Tr[γ V_NL(D)]` for an arbitrary D, using only
  the stored projectors `Pskna`, so it reads no orbitals
- `pseudopot::static_D_cv_only` (new) → the `ex_cvij` half of
  `Dnn_atom_static`; `dion` = `Dnn_atom_view() − that`; `∫V_loc·Q̂` =
  `static_h0_D() − Dnn_atom_view()`
- local potential by difference (there is no local-only H0 flag). A real check
  guards the differencing: the three pieces must reproduce
  `Tr[γ V_NL(static_h0_D)]` assembled in one shot, to 1e-9 relative.

The driver also now prints `Hartree energy = E_HF − E_x` explicitly.

### The term mapping (this is the part that is easy to get wrong)

The two codes split "one-body" vs "Hartree" vs "exchange" differently. The
mapping below is read off the ABINIT sources, not guessed:

- `e_corepsp = ecore/ucvol` with `ecore = (Σ zion)·(Σ epsatm) = N_el·Σ_a epsatm_a`
  — `64_psp/m_pspini.F90:708` (`ecore=charge*esum`). ABINIT sets
  `vpsp(G=0) = 0` and carries the α_Z term here; CoQui carries it inside
  `pp_local(G=0)`, so the two are only comparable *jointly with* `local_psp`.
- `eint(PAW) = e_kinetic + e_hartree + e_xc + e_localpsp + e_corepsp + epaw +
  e_fock + e_ewald` — `44_abitypes_defs/m_energies.F90:550-567`.
- `epaw = e1xc + eh2/2 + e1t10 − exccore − etild1xc + … + efock + …`
  — `65_paw/m_paw_denpot.F90:1184`. Note the **factor ½ on eh2**.
- The printed `xc` for PAW is `e_xc + hybcomp + e_fock`, i.e. it already
  contains the smooth Fock — `67_common/m_common.F90:1675`.

| ledger row | ABINIT | CoQui |
|---|---|---|
| kinetic | `kinetic` | `Tr[γT]` |
| local + α_Z + descreening | `localpsp + corepsp` | `Tr[γV_loc] + Tr[γ∫V_loc·Q̂]` |
| frozen one-centre D⁰ | `e1t10` | `Tr[γ dion]` |
| Hartree | `hartree + eh2/2` | `E_HF − E_x` |
| exchange | `fock0 + efock` | `E_x + Tr[γ ex_cvij]` |
| Ewald | `ewald` | (added externally by the fitter) |

**`ex_cvij` must be moved across.** CoQui keeps the frozen core-valence exact
exchange in the static D, so it lands in `e_1e`; ABINIT accounts it as
`dijfock_cv`, inside `efock`. Comparing `e_1e` without moving it mis-attributes
~0.52 Ha.

**Divergence treatment must be matched or removed on both sides.** CoQui's
`gygi` adds `−madelung·N_el/2`; what ABINIT's `fock_icutcoul 3` does to the
`q+G=0` term is *not* the same thing, and in 2026-07 it took a dedicated
`ABI_FOCK_NOSINGULAR` patch to switch off. Since the correction is analytic and
identical in form, the clean protocol is to compare *bare* exchange on both
sides and add the correction back afterwards.

### NC control

The same ledger is run for `Si_GGA_noNLCC.psp8`, where CoQui already reproduces
the reference EOS. Agreement there validates the ledger itself before it is used
to indict the PAW path. For NCPP the mapping collapses: `Tr[γ dion]` ↔
`non_local_psp`, and `ex_cvij`/`e1t10`/`eh2` are all zero.

## §2b The divergence conventions, settled from source

Neither code omits the `q+G=0` term, and they do not use the same constant. Both
must be removed analytically before exchange can be compared.

- **CoQui `gygi`**: `HF_K_correction = -madelung*S*Dm*S`, contributing
  `E_div = -(N_el/2)*madelung`, with `madelung` = the true supercell Madelung
  constant (`utils::madelung`).
- **ABINIT `fock_icutcoul 3`** = `CRYSTAL` mode in `62_poisson/m_barevcoul.F90`,
  which sets `barev(q+G=0) = i_sz = 4*pi*7.44*q0_vol^(-2/3)` with
  `q0_vol = (2*pi)^3/(nkpt_bz*ucvol)` and 7.44 the **fcc** BZ geometry factor —
  a spherical average of `4*pi/q^2` over the BZ micro-cell, contributing
  `E_div = -(N_el/2)*i_sz/(ucvol*nkpt_bz)`. It is NOT "no correction".

Both are **exactly** proportional to 1/a (`E_div*a` is bit-constant across the
six volumes: -3.759324 for ABINIT, -4.584862 for CoQui), so they differ by a pure
constant factor 1.2196 and neither can produce a curvature error. CoQui's is the
textbook leading-order finite-size correction; ABINIT's `CRYSTAL` is a cruder
approximation to the same divergence. **Do not "fix" CoQui towards ABINIT here.**

Both formulas are confirmed *numerically* by the NC control below: the bare-exchange
row agrees to 9 µHa, which is only possible if both were removed correctly.

(Aside: the `i_sz = 1832.418` recorded on 2026-07-16 came from ABINIT's **GW**
`vcoul_init` path, not `barevcoul`. The two are different conventions; do not mix
them.)

## §3b The PAW ledger, all six volumes — and the root cause

The convention-free comparison (`E_1e + E_H + E_x` with both divergence terms
removed; `ex_cvij` cancels since both codes contain it, just in different rows):

    a       ABINIT       CoQui      CoQui-ABINIT
    10.05  1.4315686   1.3228936    -0.1086750
    10.25  1.2517407   1.1344146    -0.1173261
    10.55  1.0013097   0.8712647    -0.1300450

    DRIFT 10.05->10.55 = -0.021370 Ha    slope = -0.042863 Ha/Bohr

That slope is **exactly** the +0.0430 Ha/Bohr the EOS was missing (§1). The ledger
captures the entire defect.

Splitting it needed one more number, `Tr[γ ex_cvij]`, which CoQui keeps in `e_1e`
while ABINIT accounts it in `efock`. It was already on disk: the a=10.25 run has
both a normal and an `mf_nocv` (ex_cvij-stripped) conversion, so
`Tr[γ ex_cvij] = e_1e(mf) − e_1e(mf_nocv) = 2.228274 − 2.746858 = −0.518584 Ha`.
With that:

    row        CoQui       ABINIT      diff
    ONE-BODY  +2.746858   +2.872469   -0.125611   <-- 94% of the offset
    HARTREE   +0.585739   +0.585739   +0.000000   <-- exact, at ALL six volumes
    EXCHANGE  -2.198182   -2.206468   +0.008285
                                      ---------
    TOTAL                             -0.117325

**Hartree and exchange are fine. The defect is one-body.** (E_H agreeing to
sub-µHa at every volume also exonerates the compensation charge Q̂, becsum, and the
augmentation on the Hartree side.)

### Root cause: a missing PAW-XML L=0 normalization in abinit2coqui

PAW-XML stores every radial function as its **L=0 spherical-harmonic expansion
coefficient**, i.e. √(4π) times the physical radial function. ABINIT divides each
local-potential variant by `sqrt(fourpi)` on read — `shared/libpaw/src/m_pawpsp.F90`
lines 3730 (`blochl_local_ionic_potential`), 3745 (`kresse_joubert…`), 3767
(`zero_potential`). `abinit2coqui` did this for `ae_core`/`ps_core` but **not** for
the potentials. Two consequences, one root cause:

1. **`pp_local`**: without the factor, the tail of
   `blochl_local_ionic_potential` reads `−14.17963/r` instead of `−4.0/r`. 14.18
   looks like Si's *bare nuclear* charge Z=14 — so the code concluded the
   potential was core-unscreened and **added a frozen-core Hartree V_H[ae_core]**
   (+10.00006/r). The two errors nearly cancel in the long range, leaving
   `Qtail = 4.17957` instead of 4 (+4.49%), and not at all at short range or at
   G=0: α_Z came out **25.132** Ha·Bohr³/atom against ABINIT's `epsatm = 8.858`.
   (`pp_local(G=0)·Ω = 50.264012 = 2 × 25.132006` — exactly the h5 value.)

2. **`vhtnzc`** (`reconstruct_vhtnzc`, feeding `assemble_dij0` → `dion` and the
   exported `vloc_ps`): the same missing factor on `zero_potential`, worth up to
   **6 Ha (131%)** inside the augmentation sphere and up to 12 Ha on a `dij0`
   element.

Fixes, each independently verified:

- `vion = blochl_local_ionic_potential/√(4π)`, no core-Hartree addition, and the
  Coulomb split done with the **exact** `zval` rather than the numerical plateau.
  → `Qtail = 4.000000` exactly, and α_Z = **8.858488 vs ABINIT 8.858424 (5 ppm)**.
- `vhtnzc`: use `blochl_local_ionic_potential/√(4π)` **directly** — that is what
  ABINIT itself does when the dataset provides it (`m_pawpsp.F90:3716-3730`,
  `vlocopt=2`); keep the `zero_potential/√(4π) + poisson[…]` reconstruction as the
  fallback (`vlocopt=0`, `m_pawpsp.F90:3749-3767` + `:2233-2282`). The two routes
  are now **cross-checked against each other at every conversion**: they agree to
  2×10⁻⁵ relative on both Si datasets, versus 131% before.

### Why every previous check missed it

- **The tail check passed.** `reconstruct_vhtnzc`'s `−zval/r` asymptote comes
  entirely from its poisson term, so it is *completely insensitive* to a √(4π)
  error in `zero_potential`. The B2 validation checked exactly that tail.
- **`pp_local`'s tail was 4.17957, only 4.5% off** — small enough to read as
  "dataset noise", which is what `Qtail` was designed to absorb.
- **It looks like a constant.** The B-tests recorded this as a "benign vloc-G=0
  gauge constant" of 4 occ × 35.71 mHa, benign *because it is constant at fixed
  volume*. That is precisely the wrong test for an equation of state: the term is
  ∝1/Ω, so a constant offset at one volume is a slope error across a series.
- **NC is immune** — the norm-conserving path reads psp8, not PAW-XML — so every
  NC validation stayed clean, which is also what made the NC control decisive.

## Gotchas collected

- Both `eos_exx` and `eos_exx500` use `Psdj_paw_pbe_std/Si.xml`, **not** the EOS
  series' `jth_with_d/Si.xml`. They are not a reference for this comparison.
- `eos_conv500_coqui` (std dataset, 6 volumes) predates the `V_LL` fix, so its
  `E_c` (−0.61…) is unusable; its `e_1e` is `V_LL`-independent and still valid.
- The fitter (`eos_exxrpa_fit.py`) refuses monotone / underdetermined fits.
  Trust the refusal — it caught a bogus a0 = 10.98 at "0.000 mHa residual".
- Validate an EOS on **B0/B'**, never a0: pre-`V_LL`-fix a0 was 10.2293 (within
  0.015 of reference) while B0 was 45 GPa against 98.
